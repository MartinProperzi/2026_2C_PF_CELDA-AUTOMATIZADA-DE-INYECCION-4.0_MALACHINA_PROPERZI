drawing machine, arduino based by muellerr_ch on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4755063

Summary:
this is a drawing machine based on an arduino uno. it is similar to other designs available on thingiverse. i chose to design my own because most available machines do not hide away the electronics and the motors. all in all i was surprised how easily such a plotter can be built. operating the plotter is also easy when using migrbl that can drive the servo on the z axis. i generate most gcode either with inkscape, carbide create or with my own python scripts (e.g. to generate cycloid drawings).components used:1x arduino uno1x arduino cnc shield version 32x stepper drivers a4988 (soon to be replaced with silent tmc22092x nema 17 stepper motors4x 8x500 mm carbon rods8x 8x12x15 mm brass bushings2x 20 teeth, 5 mm hole pulleys5x 20 teeth, 5 mm hole pulleys with ball bearings1x timing belt gt2, 6 mm wide, around 2.5 m longvarious 3 mm hex boltsi bought almost all component online in china.more information can be found here:https://www.muellerr.ch/arduino-drawing-machine/video of the drawing machine in action:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujbIIkblkWw
